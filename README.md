## [2026 Startup Funding Directory](https://kartik4042.github.io/StartupDirectory/index.html)

A single-file HTML table of **210 early-stage startups** funded between January and August 2026.

## Features

- **Search** by company name, description, or domain
- **Filter** by domain (AI/ML, FinTech, Health, etc.) or funding round
- Color-coded **domain** and **round** badges for quick scanning
- Columns: Company · What They Do · Domain · Amount · Round · Date
